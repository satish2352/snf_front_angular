Social Networking Forum

While social media was used to spread religious and political unrest on a large scale, "Social Networking For Social Cause" movement was founded by Pramod Gopalrao Gaikwad in the year 2010 on Facebook. This informal movement was later on instituionalised as 'Social Networking Forum'. The movement has received great response from the social net-workers across the world and through this initiative, hundreds of projects are undertaken and implemented.
